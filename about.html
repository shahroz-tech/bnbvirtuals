Our Story

About BnB Virtuals

We're a team of short-term rental specialists on a mission to help hosts reclaim their time and grow their income — without the burnout.

Who We Are

Built by Hosts, for Hosts

BnB Virtuals was founded with a simple observation: short-term rental hosts were spending more time managing their properties than actually enjoying the income from them. Guest messages at 2am, chasing cleaners, responding to reviews, monitoring five different platforms — the grind was real.

So we built a team of virtual assistants who are exclusively trained in serviced accommodation — not generalist freelancers, not call centre agents, but specialists who understand Airbnb algorithms, OTA dynamics, pricing tools, and guest psychology.

Today, BnB Virtuals supports hosts across the UK and beyond, managing hundreds of properties from single rooms to multi-property SA portfolios — all remotely, all professionally, all hands-off for you.

Our Process

How We Onboard You

From first call to fully running — in less than a week.

  • 1

    Discovery Call

    A free 30-minute call to understand your properties, your goals, your current processes, and your biggest pain points.

  • 2

    VA Matching

    We select the best VA from our trained pool based on your property type, location, and guest demographic.

  • 3

    Tailored Briefing

    Your VA is fully briefed on your properties, your brand tone, your house rules, and your preferences before going live.

  • 4

    Go Live & Monitor

    Your VA takes the reins. We check in weekly and send performance reports — you stay in control without doing the work.
